How it works

Three steps, one form.

Each step takes less than a minute. Your record is stored against your email — the secretariat picks it up from there.

STEP 01

Submit your details.

Title, full name, province, diocese, position, travel logistics and a passport photo. Cascading province / diocese so you only see the right options.

STEP 02

We plan around you.

The host secretariat uses your details to schedule airport pickups, assign rooms, print your badge and brief the protocol team.

STEP 03

Look up any time.

Return with the same email and check your accreditation status, room and pickup time. No login required.

What the host secretariat uses this for

Planning,
not paperwork.

Submissions go straight to the secretariat of the host diocese. Nothing is shared outside the planning team. The information you give is used for accreditation, accommodation, transport and protocol — the four operational systems of the host.

Accreditation

Badges and ID cards printed for every approved delegate.

Accommodation

Hotels and rooms allocated to your stay, with check-in support.

Airport pickup

Vehicle and driver assigned to your arrival date and time.

Protocol briefing

VIP flags, dietary needs and special requirements captured early.
